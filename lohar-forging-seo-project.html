01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Learning SEO through practical work.

The Lohar Forging website was the subject of my practical SEO training. I worked on keyword research, basic on-page SEO, content optimization and backlink tracking, with a focus on industrial product content and relevant search queries.

The work included organizing a keyword list, preparing content samples and maintaining backlink records. These activities helped me connect classroom concepts with a structured SEO workflow.

02 / PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Build a thoughtful SEO foundation.

My objective was to understand how relevant keywords, readable product content and organized tracking can support website discoverability. This was a learning objective, not a claim that visibility or rankings improved.

  • Explore relevant product and location-based keyword opportunities.
  • Practice aligning headings and content with the intended search query.
  • Organize off-page activity so it could be reviewed and followed up.

03 / MY ROLE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Research. Write. Document.

As a trainee, my contribution centered on research, content preparation and record keeping.

  • Compile and organize relevant keywords for industrial product topics.
  • Practice basic on-page SEO through keyword placement, headings and internal-link planning.
  • Prepare and refine SEO content samples for eye-bolt-related topics.
  • Record backlink platforms, target pages, submission information and status.

Content drafts and recommendations are distinct from changes published on the website. No implementation, indexing or performance result is implied without supporting evidence.

04 / SEO ACTIVITIES PERFORMED

A practical working process.

01

Keyword research

Organized product and location-based terms into a keyword list, considering relevance, search intent and priority. Search-volume and difficulty estimates require a documented source before being treated as verified data.

02

Basic on-page SEO

Practiced page-title and description planning, heading structure, natural keyword placement and contextual internal linking. The focus was understanding the purpose of each element.

03

Content optimization

Prepared eye-bolt-related content samples with structured headings, product explanations and relevant questions. Reviewed clarity and keyword relevance; technical product claims require verification before publication.

04

Backlink tracking

Maintained records of backlink activity, including platforms, target URLs, anchor text, dates and status. Tracking an entry is distinct from verifying that it is live, indexed or contributing to search performance.

07 / WHAT I LEARNED

From isolated tasks to a clear process.

  • Keyword relevance and search intent matter alongside keyword selection.
  • Headings, natural keyword placement and useful internal links give content a clearer structure.
  • Industrial content needs careful fact-checking as well as readable writing.
  • Backlink records need full URLs, dates and accurate status labels to be useful.
  • Completed activities, recommendations and measured outcomes should be presented separately.
A foundation for my next opportunity

This project gave me practical exposure to SEO research, content work and documentation. I’m continuing to build these foundations as a fresher seeking internship and entry-level opportunities.
