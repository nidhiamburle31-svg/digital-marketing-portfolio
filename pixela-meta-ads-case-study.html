01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Creative execution meets campaign planning.

This personal training project explores how a local education offer could be communicated through Meta ad creatives and a structured lead-generation proposal. I created square, portrait and vertical visual formats for a Digital Marketing Course in Virar West and organized a supporting admission-growth strategy.

The presentation uses planning assumptions to demonstrate funnel thinking. It is not evidence of a live advertising account, campaign delivery or business performance.

02 / PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Plan a path from awareness to demo enquiries.

The proposed objective was to promote Pixela’s Digital Marketing Course locally, invite suitable prospects to book a free demo class, and explore how a lead funnel could support a planning target of 40 student admissions.

PLANNING TARGET · NOT AN ACTUAL RESULT40 proposed admissions

Structured as four proposed batches of ten students. The presentation’s funnel model begins with 400 qualified leads; neither figure represents achieved performance.

10 / KEY LEARNINGS

Separate the plan from the proof.

  • Creative formats should communicate the same core offer while respecting different aspect ratios.
  • Audience groups need distinct motivations and messages rather than one generic approach.
  • A clear CTA helps connect ad messaging to the intended next step.
  • Testing budgets and funnel figures are hypotheses until a campaign is launched and measured.
  • Planned metrics must never be presented as real results.
Practice for future opportunities

This project gave me beginner-level exposure to Meta Ads planning, creative design, audience research and honest performance framing as I prepare for internships and entry-level roles.
